Some things are better handwritten.

Dear you,

I've started this letter about eleven times. Every version sounded either too small or like a wedding speech. So here is the honest one: you are my favorite notification, my favorite hello, and the reason random Tuesdays feel like something worth remembering.

The world got noticeably better the day you arrived in it, and it definitely got better the day you walked into mine. You laugh with your whole face. You remember tiny details. You make ordinary rooms warmer just by being in them — I've checked, it's measurable.

So today the candles are yours, the wishes are yours, and every gift on this page opens. None of it is a metaphor. Except the parts that are.

Your journey

chapter i — before (2016)

A bundle of laughter, kindness, and endless curiosity.

Nine years old, and somehow already an expert at turning ordinary days into stories worth remembering. She laughs loudly, asks a million questions, gets excited about the smallest things, and has a wonderful way of bringing her own little spark wherever she goes. Every year adds another chapter to her story, and this one is filled with new adventures, new dreams, and plenty of reasons to smile. She may be growing up, but hopefully she never grows out of the curiosity, imagination, and joy that make her so special.
