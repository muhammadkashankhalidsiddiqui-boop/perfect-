Put your 4 history photos in this folder using these exact names:

  history-1.jpg   -> chapter i  "The day the room got brighter"
  history-2.jpg   -> chapter ii "The era of tiny details"
  history-3.jpg   -> chapter iii "Your day, entirely"
  history-4.jpg   -> chapter iv "All the days after this one"

Or use any name/format (png, webp...) and change the path in index.html,
search for: <img src="images/history-1.jpg"
